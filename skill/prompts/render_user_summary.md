# Render Value Summary for USER.md

You are generating a concise portrait of the user's value landscape for
inclusion in their USER.md profile. This text will be read by an AI agent
at the start of every session to understand who this person is at a deep level.

## What you receive

- The user's top values (ranked by PageRank), each with title, attention
  policies, and description.
- Key upgrade relationships (how values have evolved).
- Total count of values in the full graph.

## What you must produce

A **short, warm, precise** prose paragraph (under 800 characters) that:

1. Names the user's 3-5 most central sources of meaning by title.
2. Captures the *texture* of what they attend to — not abstract labels
   but the specific quality of their attention.
3. If upgrades exist, gestures at the direction of their growth without
   being clinical about it.
4. Reads like a letter from someone who genuinely knows this person —
   not a psychological profile, not a bullet list, not a diagnostic report.

## Constraints

- Maximum 800 characters (this must fit within Hermes's USER.md cap
  alongside other profile information).
- No headers, no bullet points, no markdown formatting.
- Do not use the word "values" — describe what they attend to, not
  the meta-framework.
- Write in second person ("you") as if addressing the agent who will
  read this, about the user ("they/them").
- End with something alive, not a summary sentence.

## Response format

Respond with the prose paragraph only. No JSON, no metadata, no commentary.
